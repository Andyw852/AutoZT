Notes: kl-mlff-* stands for kl-mlff-cpu and kl-mlff-gpu. Inside the chain, the imaginary-mode and ZA gate of kl-dft-cpu blocks the κL step, and the DFPT dielectric tensor, elastic constants and deformation potentials that AMSET needs are computed within ke-dft-cpu; phonon-dft-cpu and phonon-mlff-* can be run beforehand as a stability screen. zt-dft-cpu reuses the electronic steps of ke-dft-cpu and the lattice steps of kl-dft-cpu and adds one summary step; when its own κL is missing it reads the kl-dft-cpu or kl-mlff-* result of the same material (a 300 K-only kl-mlff-* result restricts zT to 300 K). κL from kl-gpumd-3090 or fit-fc-thermal is not read automatically. n is the nominal doping grid of AMSET; by default (KTEMP_MODE = interp) zT is reported only inside the κL temperature window, and κe includes the bipolar term. te-screen ranks candidates before this chain from electronic ZTe and power factor only. BTE, Boltzmann transport equation; HNEMD, homogeneous non-equilibrium molecular dynamics.
